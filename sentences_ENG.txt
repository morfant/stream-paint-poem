Some-bodies are listening, too/


Something has been changing/

An old persimmon tree nearby/

In May, its tiny yellow flowers used to fall 
sounding just like real rain/

Beneath the tree
stray cats came to drink water/ 

in white bowls/

Into the empty bowl
when a flower fell, it rang softly/ 

How's the weather there?/ 

Are you there?/ 

Some-bodies are listening, too/

I am sitting on the stone wall 
with two silicone ears/
  
letting the sound flow somewhere/
 
Six years passed/

Waiting/
 
forgetting that I’m waiting/

Still, I remain/  

Every dawn
a burst of birds’ first calls/ 

It is coming closer/ 

It is about to pour/

How much time has passed?/

It will continue/ 

disconnecting 
and then reconnecting/

How wide is the window open?/

Wind by window/

Can you see it?/ 

It’s passing through
hitting against itself/

Did you hear it?/

Attend to one thing
what your eyes hold now/

Some-bodies are listening, too/

I’m standing in front of a tunnel
 both ends open/ 

making the inside resonate/

Drip drop
Drip drop/

Water drops falling/ 

Knock knock knock/

As if tapping on the door 
of time/ 

Dripping and ringing/

Strange, isn’t it?/

I’ve lived in Georo for 87 years 
Georo means ‘beside the road’/

Yang, my family name/

In my youth, I was a coachman 
guiding a carriage down this road/
 
They say there was once a temple 
with a spring that never ran dry/

When the new road was built
both the temple and the spring were buried/ 

Yet the spring’s water still flows
seeping through the tunnel walls/

Look at this water drop/

I heard that story
 from an old woman/
 
She was struck by a car
and died on the new road/ 

The road is called Beonyoung-ro
meaning ‘thriving road’/

In Georo, we have many memorial stones 
placed to remember/

wishing memory as firm as stone/

Nonetheless the moss settled on the stone 
growing little by little/

eroding it/

My face, weathered and worn
the stone, covered in green/

My face, a memory/

The sound of words scatters/

Where do they all disappear to?/

Some-bodies are listening, too/

Sound needs a space to resonate
to be heard/

Is this the space?/
 
Each listener brings their own time
like something from far away/

Time seems to flow differently 
or perhaps it’s not flowing at all/

Maybe the idea of flowing is an illusion/

Only we listen/ 

Once again
we dip our ears into the stream/

It’s never the same stream/
